Research Projects
======

* **Jan. 2025 - Dec. 2027**, Principal Investigator, National Natural Science Foundation of China Young Student Basic Research Project (Doctoral Students), “因果机器学习与结构模型估计的结合理论研究与优化应用” (*Integrating Causal Machine Learning and Structural Model Estimation: Theory and Optimization Applications*).
* **Jan. 2023 - Dec. 2027**, Participant, National Natural Science Foundation of China Major Project, “供应链韧性与安全基础理论” (*Theoretical Foundations of Supply Chain Resilience and Security*). Related research: [Metrics of Supply Chain Resilience]({{ base_path }}/publication/2025-supply-chain-resilience-metrics).
* **Feb. 2025 - Present**, Participant, State Grid Shanghai Electric Power Company Management Consulting Project, “基于SPOT框架的电网供应链网络韧性与安全分析” (*Power Grid Supply Chain Network Resilience and Security Analysis Based on the SPOT Framework*).
* **Dec. 2025 - Dec. 2026**, Participant, China Energy Research Society Research Consulting Project, “光伏行业四层级结构模型构建服务项目” (*Four-tier Structural Model Construction Service Project for the Photovoltaic Industry*). Related research: [Competition, Overcapacity, and Consolidation in a Multi-Tier Solar Manufacturing Chain]({{ base_path }}/publication/2025-photovoltaic-capacity-decision).
